# Vision

A social reading web app where readers log the books they read with comments, reviews and labes, build a steady reading habit with goals and read time, and read together with friends and book clubs.

## Vision statement

For readers who want to read more and share it with others, our app is a web platform that turns reading into a social, trackable habit. Unlike a simple reading list, it combines reading sessions, reading day streaks, read challenges and book clubs. This way readers stay motivated and connected to other users to push themselves on reading habit using gamification, competitions and shared opinions.

## Target users

- **Casual readers** who want to build a habit and see their progress.
- **Avid readers** who log many read books, write reviews on them and chase challenges.
- **Social readers** who read with friends or in a club and want to talk about it.

## Goals

1. Make it easy to record what you read and what you think of a book.
2. Keep readers coming back through sessions, streaks and achievements.
3. Connect readers through friends, messages and clubs.

## Features beyond the MVP

The full vision adds richer social features and smart discovery on top of the MVP.

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Real-time chat | Book clubs and reading challenges have a real-time chat where every member can send messages visible to the whole group. The chat supports text, images and videos. |
| Reading journal | Users read a book, mark it as read in their catalog and add comments, reviews and descriptions, private or public to other users. This keeps a record of their reaction to every book they have read. |
| Recommendations | The app suggests new books based on the taste of similar users who read similar books, friends' reading, the user's ratings, genres and more. |
| Reading sessions and streaks | Measure reading speed, mark the page where the last session stopped and keep a record of every minute read, to see how much time goes into reading. A streak counts the consecutive days the user has read. |
| Club discussions | Discussion threads inside a club that members can keep and come back to. |
| Custom challenges | Users create their own challenges for a friendly competition on who reads more. The chat boosts engagement: competing with friends motivates reading and builds a healthy habit. |
| Badges, achievements and yearly retrospective | Users earn badges and achievements, including secret missions such as read 1000 minutes, read 10 books or read 3 fiction books. Each year they also get a retrospective of what they read. |
| Shelves and reading status | Save books to folders the user creates and tag them as want to read, in progress or finished. |
| Friends | Add other users as friends. |
