# Vision

A social reading web app where readers log what they read, build a steady reading habit, and read together with friends and book clubs.

## Vision statement

For readers who want to read more and share it with others, our app is a web platform that turns reading into a social, trackable habit. Unlike a simple reading list, it combines reading sessions, streaks, challenges and clubs, so readers stay motivated and connected.

## Target users

- **Casual readers** who want to build a habit and see their progress.
- **Avid readers** who log many books, write reviews and chase challenges.
- **Social readers** who read with friends or in a club and want to talk about it.

## Goals

1. Make it easy to record what you read and what you think of it.
2. Keep readers coming back through sessions, streaks and achievements.
3. Connect readers through friends, messages and clubs.

## Features beyond the MVP

The full vision adds richer social features and smart discovery on top of the MVP. Three items are already agreed; the rest are proposals.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Photos and videos in chat | Send images and videos in one-to-one and club conversations. | MVP-09 | Agreed |
| AI book recommendations | Suggest books based on reading history, ratings and favourite genres. | MVP-03, MVP-04 | Agreed |
| Reading pace | Measure reading speed in pages per minute from reading sessions, show how it changes over time and estimate the time left to finish a book. | MVP-05, MVP-07 | Agreed |
| Club discussions | Group chat and chapter by chapter discussion threads inside a club, with a reading schedule. | MVP-10 | Proposed |
| Custom challenges | Users and clubs create their own challenges (e.g. 20 books this year, read 3 classics). | MVP-12 | Proposed |
| Yearly reading goal | Set a goal of books or pages for the year and track progress. | MVP-07 | Proposed |
| Activity feed | See what friends are reading, finishing and reviewing. | MVP-08 | Proposed |
| Quotes and highlights | Save favourite passages from a book and share them. | MVP-03 | Proposed |
| Notifications | Alerts for friend requests, messages, club activity and a streak about to break. | MVP-06, MVP-08 | Proposed |
| Mobile app | Native or installable (PWA) app with offline reading sessions. | MVP-05 | Proposed |
